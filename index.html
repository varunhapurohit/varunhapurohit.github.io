Description FAQ

Questions?
Answers!

What types of projects do you take on?

I work with startups and businesses on branding, web design, and development projects. Whether you need a full brand identity or a new website, I can help.

How long does a typical project take?

Sprint projects (branding or website) typically take 2–4 weeks. Larger engagements are scoped individually based on complexity and requirements.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes — the Design & Dev on Demand subscription gives you ongoing access to design and development work on a flexible monthly basis.

What is your pricing model?

Sprint packages are fixed-price. On Demand is subscription-based. Template support is the most affordable option for early-stage projects.

Can you work with my existing team?

Absolutely. I collaborate well with in-house teams, developers, and other freelancers. I can slot into your workflow or lead the project end-to-end.

Where are you based?

I work remotely and am available for clients worldwide. Time zone overlap is typically not an issue — I'm flexible with scheduling.
